# Soundify 0.4.0

A free, offline, ad-free Android stem mixer and Clean Video music remover.

## Clean Video

- **Chunk scheduling fixed.** Files whose final chunk was longer than the 5 s hop scheduled one more chunk without an overlap tail and failed with `missing overlap tail`. 5 of the 37 test clips hit it. The chunk plan is now built up front and stops at the final range, so every duration completes.
- **Separation runs at the model's trained input level again.** The MRX encoder's BatchNorm statistics come from audio normalised to -27 LUFS. Since 0.3.0 the app separated at native level, which put phone audio roughly 13 dB outside that range. Each file is now measured once, scaled to -27 LUFS for inference, and the stems are divided by the same gain afterwards. The clean mix is still matched to -16 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP.
- **Measured effect:** on the 33 completed clips of the test corpus, voice levels stayed within ±0.5 dB while the music stem became 2–4 dB more prominent on most music-heavy clips. The separation quality ceiling of the bundled model is unchanged; a checkpoint bake-off is still planned.
- **Residual assignment** is implemented (`PASS` / `MUSIC` / `MUSIC_SFX`) but left off until it is measured on real clips.
- **History.** Finished runs are stored under Soundify's Clean Video folder with a source fingerprint, name, duration, stems and size. Selecting a video that was already cleaned offers the existing result instead of separating it again. Results can be reopened, saved, deleted or cleared from the history screen.
- **In-app storage.** Settings and the history screen show a byte breakdown (projects, Clean Video results, temporary files, app and model) and a **Free up space** action. It removes temporary files and Clean Video results; projects and files saved through the system picker are kept.

## Known limitations

- Separation quality depends on the mix. Music with vocals, heavy compression or loud effects can still bleed between stems; the bundled MRX model is the limiting factor.
- A 20-minute video still takes roughly 25–30 minutes on the tested phone.
- The APK is debug-signed (personal build). Future releases must keep the same signing key to update in place.
- Stem Mixer requires all stems in a project to share the same sample rate.
- MP3/AAC stems may need a manual offset because of encoder delay.
- The source video is needed to save a video from a history entry. If the original URI is no longer readable, audio saving still works.
